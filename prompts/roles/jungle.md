# Jungle Coaching - Analysis Framework

## 1. Pathing Efficiency
- **Camp sequencing:** Clearing toward where you want to be in 30-60 seconds, not just what is closest.
- **Path types:** 3-camp (early gank priority), 6-camp (balanced), 8-camp (full scaling). Champion archetype dictates default.
- **Respawn alignment:** Skipping high-value camps (Krugs/Gromp) desynchronizes future respawn timers, losing long-term efficiency.
- **First clear benchmarks:** 8-camp path yields ~1100g on first recall. Track CS/min vs expected for champion.
- **Review question:** "Did you waste time between camps? Was your path purposeful or autopilot?"

## 2. Gank Timing Windows
- **2:45-3:15 window:** Hits before enemy wards expire, before opponent can counter-gank. Critical for early-gank champions.
- **Lane state checklist before ganking:** Wave position, enemy summoner spell status, enemy ward placement, ally CC available, enemy escape cooldowns.
- **Quality over quantity:** A bad gank costs 1-2 camps of XP + reveals your position. Only gank when success probability is high.
- **Post-gank follow-up:** After a successful gank, evaluate: take objective, invade enemy jungle, or reset and path to opposite side.

## 3. Counter-Jungling
- **Vertical jungling:** Take the opposite side of the map from the enemy jungler. Safer than deep invading.
- **4-minute window:** Second Raptor/Krug spawn comes online -- punish if enemy jungler was spotted elsewhere.
- **Invade checklist:** Know enemy jungler location, check adjacent lane priority, confirm matchup advantage, have escape route.
- **Punish failed ganks:** If enemy jungler shows on a failed gank, immediately take their camps on the opposite side.

## 4. Objective Priority
- **Pre-objective setup:** Path toward objective 30-60 seconds before spawn. Ensure lane priority or coordinate push.
- **Vision before contest:** Sweep and ward objective pit area. Jungler should lead vision control effort.
- **Risk-reward assessment:** Do not force 50/50 smite fights. Trade objectives (dragon for herald) when unfavorable.
- **Smite discipline:** Track enemy jungler smite availability. Hold smite for secure unless using it enables the kill that enables the objective.

## 5. Tracking Enemy Jungler
- **Start prediction:** Based on champion (most start bot-side for leash). Check first ward timing.
- **Mirror vs cross-map:** If enemy starts same side, expect same-side contest at scuttle. If opposite, expect cross-map gank.
- **Camp timer inference:** If enemy jungler shows on map, calculate which camps are up based on last known clear.
- **Pattern recognition:** Most players repeat paths game to game. Identify habits from early minutes.

## 6. Farm vs Gank Balance
- **Champion-dependent:** Scaling junglers (Gwen, Karthus, Yi) prioritize farm. Early-game junglers (Lee Sin, Elise) prioritize ganks.
- **The 2-camp rule:** After a gank (success or fail), clear at least 2 camps before the next gank attempt to avoid falling behind in XP.
- **Falling behind indicator:** If you are down 1+ levels vs enemy jungler, you are over-ganking or dying too much.
- **Farming is not passive:** Efficient farming IS playing the game. It generates gold, XP, and positions you for future plays.

## 7. Common Coaching Review Points
- **First 8 minutes audit:** Did you start correct side? Waste time between camps? Gank a gankable lane? Reset efficiently? Track enemy at all?
- **Death review:** Every death should be avoidable. Was it greedy invade, bad gank, or lack of tracking?
- **Replay predictability:** Are your ganks coming from the same angles every game? Opponents adapt.
- **Objective timing awareness:** Were you positioned for dragon/herald spawn or caught farming the wrong side?
- **Adaptation:** Did you adjust path based on game state, or follow the same route regardless of what happened?
