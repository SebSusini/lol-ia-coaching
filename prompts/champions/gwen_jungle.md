# Gwen Jungle - Champion Knowledge Block

## Identity
Scaling melee AP hypercarry. Farm-first jungler with elite late-game 1v9 potential. No CC pre-6.

## Clear Path
- **Standard full clear:** Blue > Gromp > Wolves > Raptors > Red (smite) > Krugs > Scuttle. Start Q (Snip Snip), take E second, Q again at 3.
- **Raptors start (advanced):** Raptors > Krugs > Red (smite) > Wolves > Gromp > Blue > Scuttle. Faster level 6 but invade-vulnerable.
- **Clear tip:** Auto-attack camps to max Q stacks (4 snips) before using Q. Zero-stack Q loses 60%+ damage.

## Power Spikes
- **Level 6:** Transforms from zero-gank to functional. R provides slow, range, and burst.
- **Nashor's Tooth (1st item):** Core spike. Enables dueling anyone and fast objective shred.
- **Nashor's + Riftmaker (2 items):** Strong 1v1 and sustained teamfight damage.
- **Rabadon's Deathcap (3rd):** Multiplicative AP spike, unlocks 1v9 potential.

## Item Build
- **Core:** Nashor's Tooth > Sorcerer's Shoes > Riftmaker > Rabadon's Deathcap
- **Situational:** Zhonya's (vs assassins), Banshee's (vs AP burst), Shadowflame (vs squishies, can replace Riftmaker)
- **Boots:** Sorc Shoes (default) > Mercury Treads (heavy CC) > Plated Steelcaps (AD assassins)
- **Runes:** Conqueror, Triumph, Legend: Alacrity, Last Stand / Magical Footwear, Cosmic Insight
- **Summoners:** Ghost + Smite (Ghost is mandatory for sticking power)

## Gank Patterns
- **Pre-6:** Only gank if kill is 90%+ guaranteed (enemy very low, laner has hard CC setup, massive overextend). Otherwise, farm.
- **Post-6:** R slow enables ganks. Flank or use E to wall-hop for angle. Ghost to run targets down.
- **Always pre-stack passive** on camps before walking into lane. Walking in with 0 stacks wastes the gank.

## Teamfight Role
- Play as melee hypercarry ADC. Hit nearest target, do not overextend for backline.
- Use W (Hallowed Mist) to block ranged abilities/CC, then shred frontline inside the zone.
- Space R casts (do not triple-cast instantly) -- auto between each R for unlock + sustained slow.
- Save Ghost for committed teamfights. W for the biggest incoming threat, not for minor poke.

## Objective Control
- Nashor's Tooth makes Gwen an excellent objective shredder (dragon, herald, baron).
- Priority: farm > steal enemy camps > dragon (if bot helps) > grubs. Can solo dragon after Nashor's.

## Matchups
- **Favorable:** Amumu, Brand, Morgana, Rammus, Zac, Sylas, Ekko -- outscale or outduel post-items.
- **Difficult:** Bel'Veth, Lillia, Master Yi, Kha'Zix, Ivern, Shaco, Nidalee, Nocturne, Rek'Sai -- early invade/snowball threats.

## Common Mistakes
1. Fighting before Nashor's Tooth -- you lose most pre-item duels.
2. Using Q with 0-2 stacks instead of stacking 4 autos first (loses 60%+ damage).
3. Forcing ganks pre-6 instead of farming toward spike -- biggest mistake by far.
4. Wasting W for minor trades, leaving no defense for burst/CC.
5. Triple-casting R instantly (overlaps slows, loses chase duration, skips auto-weaving).
6. Ignoring farm discipline when laners ping for help during weak phase.
7. Not tracking enemy jungler during vulnerable early clears.
